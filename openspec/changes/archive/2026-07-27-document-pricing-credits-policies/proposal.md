# Proposal: document pricing, credits and public policies

## Problem

The repository described billing at a high level but did not provide complete fillable pricing fields, a detailed credit lifecycle or a dedicated public policy center.

## Goal

Add documentation that lets future owners fill real values after benchmarks, guides implementation of an auditable credit system and defines all public policy pages without pretending the company or legal texts are already finalized.

## Non-goals

- choosing prices;
- choosing tax rates;
- publishing legal terms;
- enabling payments;
- implementing the frontend or backend.
