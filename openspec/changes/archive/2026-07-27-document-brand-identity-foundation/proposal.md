# Proposal: document brand identity foundation

## Motivation

The previous logo was rejected and removed. The repository needs an explicit pending state, a controlled asset tree and rules preventing agents from recreating or silently replacing the brand.

## Scope

- add the `brand/` asset structure;
- add brand governance documentation;
- enforce text-only temporary branding;
- remove legacy logo use from internal prototypes;
- update repository validation and navigation;
- separate application documentation from private company and institutional-site documentation.

## Non-goals

- design or approve the final logo;
- choose the final wordmark spelling, palette or typography;
- modify the separate institutional-site repository.
